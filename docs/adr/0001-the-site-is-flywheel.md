# The site is Flywheel

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-09

## Context and problem statement

The site needed a name before its home page could say what it is. The
site design's open question offered MadSwan, a new name, or Chuck's own
name, and the home page carried only a tagline.

## Decision drivers

- The site is the Method's home and publishes the fleet's measurements;
  its name should say what the reader is looking at.
- Nothing commercial goes on the site (`CLAUDE.md`); products appear only
  as a waitlist.
- The flywheel is already the name of the work loop, of the original
  plugin and of Flywheel Next, and the original site's animated flywheel
  becomes this site's hero.

## Considered options

- Flywheel
- MadSwan
- A new name
- Chuck's own name

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "Flywheel", because the site is about the flywheel, the
Method that turns it and the fleet it runs, and the name is already in use
for the loop and its tools. MadSwan is the company and belongs on the
commercial side, which the site keeps off its pages. A new name would have
to be taught before it meant anything. A personal name would make the site
a blog, and the site's voice is "we", a fleet and the people running it.

The domain is a separate decision, still open.

### Consequences

- Good: the home page, the hero and the Method's statement share one
  name and one figure.
- Bad: "Flywheel" is a common word and a common product name, so search
  and the domain will take more care.
